## Visual and media review

The actual [Studio screenshot](media/studio.png), [sample MP4](media/demo.mp4), [README GIF](media/demo.gif) and [self-recorded showcase](media/showcase.mp4) are generated by the real pipeline. The sample and account-like details are synthetic. Research screenshots remain local and ignored; no third-party visuals are included in these assets.

Browser review of an exported bilingual caption confirmed that the original native playback overlay covered the bottom caption. Playback/seek controls now sit outside the video. Short-screen and mobile screenshots were reviewed, including the separate frame inspector. A 390 px overflow caused by an implicit minimum grid column was corrected with an explicitly shrinkable grid track.

A hidden-radio hit-target failure was replaced with native radio inputs inside full-row labels. One repeated-export preload wait timed out during development; explicit `preload="auto"`, visible media errors and the subsequent complete flow passed. These fixes address the observed failures; they are not evidence that all browser media behavior is identical.

The release showcase includes changing a caption, choosing a frame, exporting, playing and recording again. Its generator checks that two real source recordings exist and that the second retains the edited caption, frame and stable step IDs before producing a successful showcase.

The Windows showcase is 30.9 s (MP4 811,992 bytes; GIF 3,963,029 bytes). `test-results/showcase-1793689509427/repeatability.json` confirms the two takes and retained edits. Both files were fully decoded with FFmpeg; eight sampled final-video frames were visually reviewed in `test-results/design-research/showcase-review.png` for caption readability, scene order, playback and the second take. Manual browser review also confirmed a real exported bilingual caption with the new playback bar outside the footage.

## Limits

No comparative usability study has established that the revised UI is faster or preferred. This is not a full WCAG 2.2 audit; screen-reader coverage, true browser 200% zoom and Firefox/Safari behavior remain unverified. Small windows intentionally scroll and stack controls. At short desktop heights, the preview is smaller and inspector/download sections may scroll.

The template preview is not a real-time render. Pending edits remain labeled; export is required to see them in the final video. The older [capture limits](VALIDATION.md#limits-still-unverified) and [draft recovery boundaries](VALIDATION-v0.1.1.md#limits) continue to apply. No audio, desktop automation, login reuse or natural-language planning was added.
